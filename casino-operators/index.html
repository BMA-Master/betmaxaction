A New Tournament Every Day
of the Sporting Week.

Six formats ship with the engine. Each one changes how a leaderboard is won, which changes who it appeals to and how long a contest holds attention.

Bet Max Tourney

Spreads, totals, moneylines and parlays on every game, bet free-form across the tournament window. Biggest balance at the close takes the crown. The default, and the easiest to explain.

Bet Max Longshot

Underdog wins count extra. Every winning bet at +150 or longer adds a bonus to the TD$ balance, so the board stays unresolved and players stay in to the close.

Bet Max Streaks

Wins climb Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and each tier multiplies what a win adds to the balance. The reset is what brings a player back the next day.

Bet Max Bracket

Single elimination. Each round narrows the field as bets land, and one bad bet ends a run. Produces a finale with a named winner, which gives your marketing team something to build a week around.

Bet Max Survivor

One bet per round to stay alive. Get it right and roll on, get it wrong and you are out. Runs for weeks on very little content, and gives your CRM team a daily reason to message a player.

Bet Max Squads

Players group up and battle other squads on combined results. One signup arrives with the group behind it.

You set the price and you set the prize

The engine ranks the field and hands you the result. What that ranking is worth is yours to design: cash, site credit, free spins, merchandise, tickets, an experience, a loyalty tier, or a prize on a free entry. Non-cash prizing keeps the value inside your own economy, which is usually where you want it. On paid tournaments you also set the rake. The pool itself can be player-funded, guaranteed by you, or underwritten by a sponsor.
