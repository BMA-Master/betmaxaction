Just You, the Slate, and the Leaderboard.

Sports betting strategy, without the sportsbook. You are competing with a tournament balance, not a deposit. Every player is measured in TD$ (Tournament Dollars) against everyone else in the contest.

  1. 01

    Start with TD$

    Tournament Dollars are your in-game scoring balance. Not real money, never redeemable.

  2. 02
    SpreadHome-3.5-110
    TotalOver42.5-110
    MoneyAway+150

    Use Game Odds

    Spreads, totals, moneylines, and props. The amount you risk is part of the strategy.

  3. 03

    Move Live

    Winning bets grow your stack. Losing bets shrink it. The leaderboard updates as games settle.

  4. 04

    Finish Highest

    The strongest final TD$ balance owns the leaderboard when the tournament ends.

The classic mode

Bet Max Tourney

Spreads, totals, and moneylines on every game. Free-form picks throughout the tournament window. Highest TD$ balance at the end takes the crown.

Bet Max Longshot

Underdog wins pay extra. Every winning bet at +150 odds or higher gets a 25% payout bonus straight to your TD$ balance. Reward the brave.

Bet Max Streaks

String wins together to tier up. Each consecutive win advances you Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum. Higher tiers multiply every payout. Lose and you reset.

Bet Max Bracket

Single-elimination tournament. Each round narrows the field as picks advance. One bad pick and your run is over.

Bet Max Survivor

One pick per round to win. Get it right and roll on. Get it wrong and you're out. Last person standing takes it.

Bet Max Squads

Team up with friends and battle other squads. Combined records, shared TD$ pools. Pure team warfare for league-wide bragging rights.
