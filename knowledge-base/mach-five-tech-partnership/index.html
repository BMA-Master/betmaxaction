Bet Max Action Partners with Mach Five Tech to Power Tournament-Based Sports Gaming Platform

Bet Max Action announces a strategic technology partnership with Mach Five Tech to design, build, and scale Bet Max Tourney and supporting platform infrastructure.

Home / Knowledge Base / Bet Max Action Partners with Mach Five Tech to Power Tournament-Based Sports Gaming Platform

On This Page

For Immediate Release

March 1, 2026 — Bet Max Action today announced a strategic product and platform partnership with Mach Five Tech to design, build, and scale a tournament-based sports gaming platform focused on fan engagement, flexibility, and rapid deployment across markets.

The partnership centers on the development of Bet Max Tourney, a full-stack tournament application, and the supporting platform infrastructure that lets Bet Max Action launch, test, and iterate quickly while supporting future integrations with sportsbooks, media partners, and international operators.

Mach Five Tech leads the product architecture and core application build, working closely with the Bet Max Action team to translate early concepts into a market-ready platform. The collaboration prioritizes execution velocity, modular design, and the ability to adapt the platform for a range of tournament formats, monetization models, and regulatory environments.

“We’re creating a new category in sports iGaming: tournament-style competition that blends strategic DFS elements with the standard sportsbook experience. Mach Five Tech built the platform infrastructure that makes this possible, handling complex tournament logic and real-time scoring while keeping the user experience seamless. Their technical expertise has been instrumental in bringing Bet Max Action to life.”

— David Reiter, Founder of Bet Max Action

The platform is designed to support tournament-based experiences that prioritize engagement and repeat play, including free-to-play and partner-driven formats, while maintaining the flexibility needed to support future commercialization and international expansion.

Platform Capabilities

Bet Max Tourney

  • Daily, weekly, monthly, and season-long tournaments
  • 15+ sports: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MMA, EPL, and more
  • Equal TD$ (Tournament Dollars) starting bankroll for every entrant
  • Spreads, totals, moneylines, and player props
  • Real-time leaderboards and tournament rankings

Bet Max Action Platform

  • Create unlimited tournaments with granular controls
  • Flexible entry fees from $5 to $10K+
  • White-label customization with branded experiences
  • Comprehensive analytics and player retention tools
  • RESTful API with webhook support and a sandbox environment

Try the app: betmaxtourney.com/app

About Bet Max Action

Bet Max Action is building a tournament-based sports gaming platform designed to create engaging, flexible experiences for fans, partners, and operators. The company focuses on innovative formats that bridge free-to-play engagement and future commercialization opportunities.

Learn more at betmaxaction.com and betmaxtourney.com.

About Mach Five Tech

Mach Five Tech is a technology consultancy focused on product architecture, platform development, and execution for early-stage and growth companies. The firm partners with teams to move ideas from concept to traction by aligning technical foundations, delivery velocity, and long-term scalability. Mach Five Tech operates under Mach Five Group, a multidisciplinary consulting and development firm offering technical, product, and growth services to ambitious teams across industries.

Learn more at machfivetech.com and machfivegroup.com.
